# Prism Bastion — itch.io Page Copy

## Short description

Program your towers with modular attack sequences in a bright geometric tower-defense game.

```html
<p><strong>Prism Bastion</strong> is a geometric tower-defense game about building attacks like programs. Instead of choosing a fixed weapon, you load modules into each tower from left to right. Projectiles, modifiers, targeting logic, trails, and triggers are compiled into a working attack sequence—and changing the order can completely change the result.</p>

<p>Guide piercing needles toward survivors, split burning projectiles across a crowd, bounce oversized blades through a lane, or deploy mines and persistent fields from nested triggers. Every tower is a small system to design, test, and refine while increasingly complex signals move toward the core.</p>

<h2>Features</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>39 combinable modules</strong> across projectiles, modifiers, static payloads, trails, and logic.</li>
  <li><strong>Order-driven construction:</strong> modules affect what comes after them, so sequencing is as important as selection.</li>
  <li><strong>Standard Mode</strong> with limited inventory, module drafts, wave rewards, tower upgrades, and economy management.</li>
  <li><strong>Creative Mode</strong> with unlimited modules and shards, adjustable run settings, and a signal-spawning console.</li>
  <li><strong>Five geometric sectors</strong>, including a guided opening mission and a multi-entrance route.</li>
  <li><strong>Five difficulty settings</strong> for relaxed experimentation or tightly optimized defenses.</li>
  <li><strong>Distinct signal mechanics</strong> including shields, splitting, armor, suppression auras, and mixed elite waves.</li>
  <li><strong>A local Defense Archive</strong> that records completed runs, statistics, discoveries, and achievements.</li>
  <li><strong>English and Simplified Chinese</strong>, with mouse, keyboard, and touch-friendly controls.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to play</h2>

<p>Choose a sector, mode, and difficulty, then deploy towers on the marked nodes. Open a tower's workshop and arrange modules into a valid sequence. Launch the next wave, study what gets through, and rebuild your programs around the threats ahead.</p>

<p>On desktop, drag modules into slots or double-click one for quick installation. On touch devices, tap to select or hold briefly and drag. Landscape orientation is recommended on mobile; supported browsers can enter fullscreen from the home screen.</p>

<p><strong>Can you compile a defense strong enough to hold the Bastion?</strong></p>

<h2>Open source</h2>

<p>Prism Bastion is open source. Follow development, report issues, or explore the code on <a href="https://github.com/szdytom/ntd" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GitHub</a>.</p>
```
